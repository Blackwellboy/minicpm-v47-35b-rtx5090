A 15.5 GB quant just tied the 21 GB one.

MiniCPM-V 4.7 (OpenBMB's new 35B-A3B vision model) on ONE RTX 5090. Official Q3 mix vs Q4_K_M, 8 vision benchmarks x 250 questions each.

Q3 wins 4, Q4 wins 3, 1 tie. Every gap under 2 points.

Initial testing 🧵
---
BF16 is 70.6 GB, so on a 32 GB card it's quant or nothing:

- AWQ on vLLM: 22 GB
- Q4_K_M on llama.cpp: 21.2 GB
- Q3 mix on llama.cpp: 15.5 GB (Q4_K_M with only the routed experts at Q3_K)

All three keep full vision and found 12/12 hidden passkeys out to 58K tokens.
---
Speed on one 5090:

Single stream: ~185-196 tok/s on every version.

Under load it's not close. AWQ on vLLM hit 2,883 tok/s aggregate at 32 concurrent requests. llama.cpp topped out around 455.

Prefill on vLLM: ~13K tok/s at 58K context.
---
Our pick so far:

Best overall: AWQ on vLLM. Top vision average (83.0 at the card's 4x vision settings) and 6x the throughput.

Best value: the Q3 mix. 5 GB smaller, same scores, perplexity only +3%.

Q4_K_M didn't earn its extra 5.7 GB in our runs.
---
Card check: OCRBench reproduces (91.6 vs 91.2 claimed).

MMStar, MMMU and MathVista came in 7-13 pts under the card on our rule-scored subsets. Part of that is the model writing long answers even with thinking off. A harness note, not a debunk.
---
Trap we hit: thinking is ON by default, and on short questions the model answers before ever closing its think block. vLLM files the answer as "reasoning" and the reply comes back empty.

24 of 40 multiple-choice items. Turn thinking off for direct answers.
---
Second trap: the official AWQ repo doesn't load as shipped. Its configs point at code files that aren't in the repo.

Copy 3 config files from the base model repo over it and it runs. Both traps are written up with fixes.
